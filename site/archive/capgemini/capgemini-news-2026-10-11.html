US suspends Capgemini from the PERM green-card program

2026-10-08 | CNBC

The US Department of Labor announced on October 8 that it would suspend Capgemini, Cognizant, Infosys, Tata, Wipro and HCL from its Permanent Labor Certification program. CNBC reports that the department would stop accepting or processing new applications. Microsoft and Adobe were also suspended, with the Labor Secretary citing active federal investigations for those two companies.

The announcement came during a broader administration crackdown on foreign-worker hiring. Microsoft disputed claims that it was replacing American employees, saying 80% of its roughly 6,000 H-1B applications in the previous fiscal year concerned existing staff. Those figures describe Microsoft, not Capgemini; the cited report does not quantify Capgemini's affected employees or confirm disruption to its client delivery.

Source: CNBC

Gartner names Capgemini "Company to Beat" for Sovereign AI Services

2026-10-10 | Capgemini announcement; underlying Gartner report dated September 9

Capgemini's October 10 announcement highlights Gartner's September 9 report naming it "Company to Beat" for Sovereign AI Services. Capgemini attributes the recognition to its consulting, coordination of sovereign technology partners, large-scale delivery and work in regulated environments. Its stated scope spans sovereign cloud and AI infrastructure through broader transformation while maintaining control, resilience and compliance.

The accompanying Gartner methodology considers technical capabilities, customer implementations, potential customer base, business model, partnerships and the surrounding ecosystem. Gartner describes the designation as analyst opinion that can change as the market evolves, not an endorsement. The announcement provides no named client results or quantified delivery outcomes, and the underlying assessment predates this week's coverage.

Source: Capgemini

HFS names Capgemini a Horizon 3 GCC Orchestrator

2026-10-08 | Capgemini

HFS Research placed Capgemini in Horizon 3, its GCC Orchestrator or Market Leader category, in its inaugural Global Capability Centers services report. The study assessed 24 providers on helping enterprises turn delivery centres into AI-enabled, outcome-driven operations. Capgemini's announcement highlights support across setup, transformation and ongoing operations, alongside flexible engagement models and multi-industry delivery experience.

An HFS analyst quoted in the announcement cites more than 200 GCC engagements and capabilities spanning operating-model design, data modernization, process redesign and industrializing generative AI. The announcement also describes agentic AI, digital twins and MLOps as transformation capabilities. It names cost and deployment benefits but supplies no numerical results for individual customers; this is analyst recognition, not a new client contract.

Source: Capgemini

Team take Suggested

Interpretation and possible follow-up, not reported facts.

Why it matters: GCC delivery can provide a route into enterprise data and AI programs.
