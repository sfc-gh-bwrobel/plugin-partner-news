Capgemini joins Anthropic's first Frontier Academy cohorts

2026-10-02 | Anthropic

Anthropic launched Claude Frontier Academy with a $100 million commitment and a target of training 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by the end of 2027. Capgemini is named among the first participating organizations, alongside Accenture, Bain, Deloitte and others. The announcement includes a Capgemini executive's endorsement of hands-on work with Anthropic engineers to strengthen client delivery.

Participants begin with in-person training and a practical assessment, followed by a 12-week residency delivering a real Claude use case at their own organization. A second assessment leads to the final credential, with the first expected in early 2027. The 10,000 target covers the entire program; Anthropic does not disclose Capgemini's cohort size or customer outcomes.

Source: Anthropic

Capgemini completes Government Solutions divestment

2026-09-30 | Capgemini

Capgemini confirmed that the sale of Capgemini Government Solutions to ITC Federal completed on September 30. This is the closing milestone for the definitive agreement announced on September 12, rather than another announcement of intent to sell. The subsidiary works with the US federal government; ITC Federal provides IT solutions and enterprise services to federal law-enforcement, homeland-security and defence agencies.

The earlier agreement announcement said Government Solutions represented 0.4% of Capgemini's global 2025 revenue and less than 2% of its US revenue. Those figures describe the divested subsidiary, not Capgemini's entire American business. The short closing release does not disclose a sale price, additional financial terms or customer-transition details.

Source: Closing release, Agreement announcement, background

Investigation examines HMRC's long-running Capgemini spending

2026-09-28 | The Register

The Register and procurement-data provider Otnox analysed HMRC's published transactions above GBP 25,000 and reported at least GBP 4.2 billion paid to Capgemini between 2014 and July 2026. The total spans 15,726 transactions, with sixteen months of data missing. This is new reporting on historical spending, not a GBP 4.2 billion contract awarded during this week.

The article traces HMRC's effort to replace the Aspire outsourcing arrangement while continuing to procure Capgemini services for critical systems and modernization. HMRC told the publication its awards complied with procurement law and government policy, and said its supplier base had become more diverse. The analysis highlights incumbent relationships but does not establish procurement wrongdoing.

Source: The Register
