### 1. Payments research puts $230 billion of bank revenue at risk
2026-09-24 | Capgemini Research Institute
Capgemini's World Payments Report 2027 projects that stablecoins, tokenized deposits and central bank digital currencies could represent approximately 4% of global payments volume by 2030, putting $230 billion of bank payments revenue at risk. The study surveyed 1,110 large corporates and 300 banking executives across nine markets. Nearly three-quarters of corporate respondents describe cross-border payments as slow, costly and unpredictable.

Banks remain the preferred provider at equivalent cost and quality, but nearly 60% of corporates would consider non-bank stablecoin services if banks lag. Only 21% of surveyed banks are actively scaling at least one of these instruments. The revenue exposure is a research projection, not a reported loss or a Capgemini sales forecast.
- Why it matters: Payment modernization creates discussions about real-time visibility, governed data and compliance across banking systems.
- Source: [Capgemini](https://www.capgemini.com/news/press-releases/banks-risk-losing-230-billion-in-payments-revenue-as-stablecoins-and-tokenized-deposits-go-mainstream/)

### 2. Sweden selects Capgemini to integrate its emergency network
2026-09-23 | The Critical Communications Review
The Critical Communications Review reported that Sweden's Civil Defense Agency signed an agreement with Capgemini to act as system integrator for SWEN, the Swedish Emergency Network. Its role includes coordinating technical solutions, quality assurance, technical project management and coordination between suppliers. The agency said integration work was under way and the program remained on schedule.

SWEN is intended to replace the TETRA-based Rakel network by 2030, using technology partly based on 3GPP-standardized 5G. Existing suppliers include Ericsson for the core network, Telia for initial pilot radio access and Airbus for migration interoperability. Further procurements remained outstanding, including the MCX platform expected around mid-2027. The article does not disclose Capgemini's contract value.
- Why it matters: A concrete public-sector integration engagement involving secure interoperability and multiple technology suppliers.
- Source: [The Critical Communications Review](https://www.criticalcommunicationsreview.com/video/news/119739/swedish-civil-defense-agency-appoints-capgemini-as-system-integrator-for-swen)
