### 1. US suspends Capgemini from the PERM green-card program
Oct 9-14 | Les Affaires, Le Figaro, TradingView/Mint and others (8 articles)
Follow-up reporting put Capgemini among eight technology companies suspended from the US PERM labour-certification program following the October 8 announcement. Le Figaro quotes the Labor Secretary saying the department would neither accept new applications nor process pending ones. The restriction concerns a route to permanent residency for sponsored employees, rather than an announcement that existing visas have been revoked.

Le Figaro reports that Capgemini obtained 360 green cards through PERM in fiscal 2025, compared with more than 3,000 at Microsoft. That historical volume does not establish how many employees the suspension affects. Capgemini had not responded to the cited reporters, and the consequences for specific delivery teams remain unconfirmed.
- Why it matters: pressure on Capgemini's US staffing and onshore delivery.
- Follow-up: ask Capgemini US alliance contacts whether joint delivery teams are affected.
- Source: [Les Affaires](https://www.lesaffaires.com/bourse/microsoft-privee-de-permis-de-residence-permanente-pour-ses-salaries-par-le-gouvernement-americain/), [Le Figaro](https://www.lefigaro.fr/economie/prives-de-green-cards-pour-leurs-employes-microsoft-et-capgemini-dans-le-viseur-de-l-administration-trump-20261009)

### 2. Gartner names Capgemini "Company to Beat" for Sovereign AI Services
Oct 10 | Capgemini
Capgemini's October 10 announcement highlights Gartner's September 9 report naming it "Company to Beat" for Sovereign AI Services. Capgemini attributes the recognition to its consulting, coordination of sovereign technology partners, large-scale delivery and work in regulated environments. The announcement provides no named client results or quantified delivery outcomes, and the underlying assessment predates this week's coverage.

### 3. Automotive research: agentic AI adoption outpaces mature governance
Oct 12-13 | Capgemini Research Institute; ZDNET, LeMagIT, Autocar Professional, ET Auto, Fortune India, CXOToday (11 articles)
Capgemini Research Institute surveyed 800 executives at automotive manufacturers, suppliers and technology providers across 12 countries, supplemented by 24 engineering-leader interviews. Among automotive OEMs and suppliers working towards AI adoption, 35% report using agentic AI in engineering and 33% are beginning pilots. Only 5% of organizations describe their AI governance as fully mature; the adoption percentages should not be read as covering every automotive firm.

The research associates stronger results with higher-quality engineering data, clearer accountability and governance. It reports leading organizations achieving two to three times greater gains in development speed, productivity and time-to-market, while highlighting disconnected engineering systems and difficulty turning vehicle telemetry into engineering action. These are survey findings, not independently verified delivery benchmarks.
- Why it matters: Capgemini frames data foundations as the blocker to scaling agentic AI - aligned with Snowflake's data + AI governance story.
- Follow-up: use as a hook for joint automotive accounts.
- Source: [Capgemini research](https://www.capgemini.com/insights/research-library/ai-in-product-development/)
