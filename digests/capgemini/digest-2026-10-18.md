## Top stories

### 1. Automotive research: agentic AI adoption outpaces mature governance
2026-10-12 | Capgemini press release; pickup by ZDNET, LeMagIT, Autocar Professional, ET Auto and Fortune India
Capgemini Research Institute published a report on October 12 surveying 800 senior automotive executives (400 OEMs, 160 tier-1 suppliers, 240 technology providers) across 12 countries in July 2026, plus 24 interviews. It reports that 33% of organizations use agentic AI in product development and 35% are piloting it, while only 5% report fully mature AI governance.

The institute says its "high-value realizers" report 17% faster development cycles and a 19% reduction in time-to-market, attributing the gap to data foundations, business cases and governance more than AI adoption itself. Only 10% have shared lifecycle accountability across their ecosystem. These are self-reported survey results, not independently verified benchmarks.
- Why it matters: Capgemini frames data foundations and governance as the barrier to scaling agentic AI.
- Follow-up: Use the findings as a discussion starter for joint automotive accounts.
