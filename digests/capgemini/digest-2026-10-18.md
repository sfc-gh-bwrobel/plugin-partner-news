## Top stories

### 1. Automotive research: agentic AI adoption outpaces mature governance
2026-10-12 | Capgemini Research Institute; original publication date from structured metadata
Capgemini Research Institute surveyed 800 executives at automotive manufacturers, suppliers and technology providers across 12 countries, supplemented by 24 engineering-leader interviews. Among automotive OEMs and suppliers working towards AI adoption, 35% report using agentic AI in engineering and 33% are beginning pilots. Only 5% of organizations describe their AI governance as fully mature; the adoption percentages should not be read as covering every automotive firm.

The research associates stronger results with higher-quality engineering data, clearer accountability and governance. It reports leading organizations achieving two to three times greater gains in development speed, productivity and time-to-market, while highlighting disconnected engineering systems and difficulty turning vehicle telemetry into engineering action. These are survey findings, not independently verified delivery benchmarks.
- Why it matters: Capgemini frames data foundations and governance as barriers to scaling agentic AI.
- Follow-up: Use the findings as a discussion starter for joint automotive accounts.
- Source: [Capgemini research](https://www.capgemini.com/insights/research-library/ai-in-product-development/)
