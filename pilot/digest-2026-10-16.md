## Top stories

### 1. US suspends Capgemini from the green-card (PERM) program
**Oct 8-9 | Reuters, Le Figaro, Time**
The US Labor Department suspended Capgemini, Microsoft, Adobe and five IT services firms from the PERM labor certification program, stopping new and pending applications. Le Figaro reports Capgemini obtained 360 green cards through PERM in fiscal 2025.
- **Why it matters:** Possible pressure on Capgemini's US delivery staffing and talent retention, which could affect US joint projects.
- **Follow-up:** Ask Capgemini US alliance leads whether any joint delivery teams or offshore-to-onshore plans are affected.
- Sources: [Le Figaro](https://www.lefigaro.fr/economie/prives-de-green-cards-pour-leurs-employes-microsoft-et-capgemini-dans-le-viseur-de-l-administration-trump-20261009), [Time](https://time.com/article/2026/10/09/us-immigration-visa-fraud-green-card-h1b-j1-microsoft-adobe/)

### 3. Research: 68% of automotive firms use or pilot agentic AI, 5% have mature governance
**Oct 12 | Capgemini Research Institute; covered by ZDNET, LeMagIT, Autocar Professional, ET Auto**
Survey of 800 automotive executives across 12 countries. Top performers credit "stronger data foundations" and governance, not AI adoption itself. 64% say vehicle-in-use data mainly informs future programs rather than improving vehicles already on the road.
- **Why it matters:** Capgemini is publicly framing data foundations and traceability as the blocker to scaling agentic AI in automotive, which fits Snowflake's data plus AI governance story.
- **Follow-up:** Use the report as a hook for joint automotive accounts in DACH (connected-vehicle data, engineering data platforms).
- Source: [Press release](https://www.capgemini.com/news/press-releases/68-of-automotive-organizations-use-or-pilot-agentic-ai-for-product-development-but-only-5-report-mature-ai-governance-2/)

### 4. Client story: UK Ministry of Defence scales ADAPT procurement tool
**Oct 12 | Capgemini**
Capgemini helped the MOD scale a unified procurement workflow app from the RAF to the Royal Navy, Army and Defence Digital. Repeat deployments took 13 weeks instead of 24, and were 30-40% cheaper.
- **Why it matters:** Shows Capgemini's reuse-and-scale delivery model in UK public sector. The capability is application/workflow, not a data platform.
- **Follow-up:** Low priority for Snowflake unless there is a related analytics or reporting workload.
- Source: [Client story](https://www.capgemini.com/news/client-stories/ministry-of-defence-scales-accelerated-define-and-procure-tool-to-modernize-and-standardize-procurement-workflows/)
